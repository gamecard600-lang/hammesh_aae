# Non-Technical section

The non-technical section follows Part-A of GSSSB Advertisement 419/202627:
- Reasoning & Data Interpretation: 30 marks
- Quantitative Aptitude: 30 marks
- Constitution of India: 10 marks
- Current Affairs: 10 marks
- Gujarati & English Comprehension: 10 marks

The app separates this from Technical while preserving the existing technical section and progress storage.

Questions in this ZIP are syllabus-aligned practice/PYQ-pattern questions. They are **not** represented as verbatim copies of the 2025 paper.
